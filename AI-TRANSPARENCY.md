# Generative AI Authorship & Provenance Transparency Notice
### The Sovereign: Acts of the Apostles™

This document outlines the scope of **Generative AI usage**, the provenance of project components, and the clear delineation between human creative authorship and machine-assisted tooling, adhering to best practices outlined by the U.S. Copyright Office, the Open Source Initiative (OSI), and community software transparency standards.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Statement of Authorship & Creative Direction

**The Sovereign: Acts of the Apostles™** is a human-conceived, designed, and directed creative work. The overarching theological framework, tabletop rules design, historical research into the Book of Acts, narrative structure, game balance mathematics, and licensing strategy are the direct intellectual creations of the project authors.

Generative AI systems (specifically Google DeepMind Gemini models within the Google AI Studio environment) were employed strictly as **accelerative developer tools** (akin to compilers, linters, advanced IDE code assistants, and syntax formatters) under active human supervision and iterative prompting.

## ⚖️ 3. Legal & Licensing Effect

1. **Enforceability of Licenses**: The human authors hold full, uncontested rights to license the software under the **GNU AGPLv3 + Commons Clause** and reserve proprietary copyright over original game assets and narrative lore.
2. **No Unaudited Output**: All AI-assisted code generation was subject to human review, architectural refactoring, strict type checking, and functional validation.
3. **Downstream Freedom**: The use of AI assistance in generating code scaffolding does not restrict your rights as granted under the project's [LICENSE.md](./LICENSE.md) or [MODDING-POLICY.md](./MODDING-POLICY.md).
