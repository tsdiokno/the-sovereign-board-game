# Modding Policy & Community Guidelines
### The Sovereign: Acts of the Apostles™

We love community creativity, custom scenarios, and modding! To keep the ecosystem healthy, fair, and fun for everyone, all mods, extensions, and community content are governed by these three simple rules:

1. **You Own Your Original Mods**: Modders retain full credit and ownership over their original storylines, custom scenario definitions, scripts, and original artwork.
2. **Non-Commercial Only (No Paywalls)**: You cannot sell your mods, lock gameplay behind paywalls, or charge access fees. (Voluntary creator tips/Patreon donations for your time are completely fine, as long as game content is not gated).
3. **Official Community Feature Permission**: By publicly sharing your mod in the community or submitting it, you grant us permission to feature, highlight, or integrate your creation or balance ideas into the official game.
