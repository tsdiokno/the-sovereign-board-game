## 🛡️ Core Rules for Brand Usage

### 1. Official & Unmodified Builds
- You may use the official name **"The Sovereign: Acts of the Apostles"** and official logos when hosting, running, or sharing an unmodified, official release of the game to accurately indicate the software's origin.

### 2. Forks, Clones & Competing Distributions (Mandatory Rebranding)
- Anyone is free under GPLv3 to copy, modify, host, or sell the codebase.
- However, if you distribute a modified build, fork, or competing commercial service, **YOU MUST COMPLETELY REBRAND**:
  - You cannot use the words *"The Sovereign: Acts of the Apostles"* or *"The Sovereign"* in your product title, domain name, or application title.
  - You must replace all official logos, header insignias, and official proprietary portrait illustrations with your own distinct branding.

### 3. Nominative Reference
- You may describe your fork or plugin using factual, nominative statements such as:  
  *"A custom scenario pack for The Sovereign: Acts of the Apostles"* or *"A board game engine based on the open-source Sovereign core."*  
  Such references must not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or official affiliation.
